ABOUT QUICKBITE

A little more joy
in choosing food.

QuickBite is a student project exploring how a simple website could make discovering meals feel easier and more enjoyable.

✳Q✦✿

THE CONCEPT

A prototype, built with care.

What if finding your next meal felt effortless?

When people are hungry, sorting through choices can feel like a chore. QuickBite imagines bringing meal ideas together in a friendly, easy-to-understand experience. Customers could discover food, compare what catches their eye, and move toward placing an order through a future version of the service.

This website presents the idea as a front-end prototype. It shows the look and purpose of a possible service; it does not place or process real orders.
